# UbuntuCare Patient Guide

For patients accessing healthcare through UbuntuCare facilities.

---

## What is UbuntuCare?

UbuntuCare is Zambia's digital health platform. It helps:
- **You**: Get faster care, receive reminders, access your health information
- **Your nurse/doctor**: Spend less time on paperwork, more time with you
- **Your community**: Better disease tracking and health resources

**"Ubuntu"** means "I am because we are" — we care for each other.

---

## Your Rights

1. **Your data is private** — Only you and your healthcare providers can see it
2. **You control your information** — You can request your records anytime
3. **Your care comes first** — AI assists, but humans make all medical decisions
4. **You can choose your language** — Content available in English, Nyanja, Bemba

## During Your Visit

### Check-In

When you arrive at the clinic:
1. Tell the receptionist your name or NRC number
2. Or use the self-service kiosk
3. You'll be added to the queue

### Consultation

Your provider may use voice technology to document your visit:
- They will ask your permission before recording
- The system transcribes your conversation
- This helps your provider focus on you, not on typing
- Your conversation is encrypted and private

### After Your Visit

- You may receive SMS reminders for:
  - Follow-up appointments
  - Medication schedule
  - Vaccination due dates
  - Health tips for your condition

## Zambia-Specific Features

### NRC Number

Your National Registration Card (NRC) number helps link your records across all facilities in Zambia. Your NRC stays private and secure.

### NHIMA Insurance

If you have NHIMA insurance:
- Your insurance is verified automatically when you check in
- Claims are submitted automatically
- No paper forms needed
- Check your NHIMA balance on the portal

### SmartCare

UbuntuCare works with SmartCare, Zambia's national health system. This means:
- Your records can be accessed at any facility in Zambia
- Emergency care providers can see your allergies and conditions
- You don't need to repeat your history at every visit

## Privacy & Security

- Your health data is **encrypted** (scrambled so only providers can read it)
- Data is stored **in Zambia** (never leaves the country)
- Only **authorized healthcare workers** can see your records
- You can **request a copy** of your records anytime
- You can **report concerns** about your data privacy

### Your Health Data Rights (Zambia Data Protection Act)

- Right to know what data we have
- Right to correct incorrect data
- Right to request deletion (with limits for clinical records)
- Right to data portability (get your data in standard format)
- Right to complain to ZICTA

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is my conversation recorded?**
A: Audio is transcribed in real-time and not stored as audio. Only the text note is saved.

**Q: Can I opt out of the AI system?**
A: Yes. Tell your provider. Your care quality will not be affected.

**Q: What if I don't have a phone?**
A: You can still access services. Ask staff for assistance.

**Q: What if I speak Nyanja or Bemba?**
A: The system supports multiple languages. Tell your provider your preference.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes. All data is encrypted and stored in Zambia. Only authorized providers can access it.

**Q: How do I get my test results?**
A: Your provider will explain them. You can also view them on the patient portal.
